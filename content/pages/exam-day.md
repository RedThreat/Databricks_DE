The ticks on this page are saved in your browser.

## When you book

- [ ] Create or log in to your account on the exam delivery platform (Webassessor) and book the exam.
- [ ] For an online proctored exam, run the system check on the computer you will use, with the same network.
- [ ] Check that the name on your account matches your ID exactly.
- [ ] Download the current exam guide again two weeks before the date and compare it with the [exam outline](#/exam/A) on this site.

## The day before

- [ ] No new material. Read the [cheat sheet](#/page/cheatsheet) once and review your missed questions.
- [ ] Prepare a clean desk: nothing on it except the computer, no second screen, no notes.
- [ ] Close every application that could start or notify during the exam; disable sleep and updates.
- [ ] Sleep.

## Right before the exam

- [ ] Valid photo ID within reach.
- [ ] Laptop on power, webcam and microphone working, stable network.
- [ ] Water if allowed; phone out of reach.
- [ ] Log in 15 to 30 minutes early.

## Pacing

| | Associate | Professional |
|---|---|---|
| Questions on screen | 45 scored plus up to 10 unscored | 60 scored plus possible unscored |
| Time | 90 minutes | 120 minutes |
| Budget per question | About 1.5 minutes | About 1.8 minutes |
| Checkpoint | Question 25 by minute 40 | Question 35 by minute 60 |

Unscored questions are not marked, so treat every question as scored. The time for them is included.

## During the exam

1. **Read the last sentence first.** It tells you what is being asked: the cause, the fix, the cheapest option, the least privilege, the one that keeps history.
2. **Find the deciding word.** "Incremental", "idempotent", "without copying", "lowest cost", "least privilege", "all current and future tables", "must not stop" usually separate two plausible answers.
3. **Eliminate.** Two options are usually wrong at a glance: commands that do not exist, a tool from another category, or a fix for a different symptom.
4. **Code questions**: check write mode, NULL handling, 0- versus 1-based indexing, `union` versus `unionByName`, a missing `groupBy`, the trigger, and which decorator or clause is used.
5. **Old names are not wrong.** DLT, Workflows, Repos and Asset Bundles may still appear; do not discard an option only because of a name.
6. **Flag and move on** after about two minutes; come back at the end. Never leave a question unanswered: there is no penalty for wrong answers.
7. **Multiple answers**: when the question says "choose two", each option must be true on its own.

## After the exam

- The result appears at the end of the exam; the certificate and badge arrive by email afterwards.
- The certification is valid for two years. The Associate can be renewed with the renewal exam or the current full exam; the Professional by passing the current exam.
- Note the topics that felt weak while they are fresh: they are the starting point for the [Professional plan](#/plan/P).
